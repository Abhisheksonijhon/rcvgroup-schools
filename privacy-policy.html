Privacy Policy

How information submitted through this website is handled.

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Shri Rameshchandra Soni Residential School, Harpalpur (“the School”, “we” or “us”) respects the privacy of website visitors. This notice explains general handling of information when you browse the site or contact the School.

Information you provide

If you contact us by phone, email, WhatsApp or the contact form, you may provide your name, phone number, email address, enquiry subject and message. Do not submit sensitive personal information unless necessary and requested through an appropriate channel.

Use and retention

Information you submit may be used to respond to enquiries, provide admission or school-related information, follow up on requests and maintain website operations. Access should be limited to authorised personnel, and information should be retained only as reasonably necessary for the purpose or applicable recordkeeping obligations.

Forms and third-party services

The contact form is marked for Netlify Forms. Actual delivery and storage depend on the hosting and form-processing configuration enabled for the deployed site. External links such as Google Maps, WhatsApp and the School’s website-development partner operate under their own privacy practices. Do not assume a form was delivered unless the site shows a clear confirmation.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

This policy does not claim that analytics or advertising trackers are active. Before enabling analytics, advertising pixels or non-essential cookies, the School should update this notice to describe the actual tools and obtain consent where required. The Aarunya Web Solutions banner is a clearly labelled promotional link, not a third-party ad-network unit.

Children’s information

Because the School serves children, student information should be handled carefully, access restricted, and unnecessary personal details avoided on public pages. Parents or guardians may contact the School about information they have submitted.

Security and requests

Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards should be applied. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. To ask about, correct or request deletion of information you submitted, contact the School; statutory school records may have separate retention requirements.
